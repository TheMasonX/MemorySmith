## Architecture Notes

1. **Validation script coverage is good but has gaps.** The 4 validated scripts (task records, memory records, page links, page path literals) are all wired into CI and the Validate-Repo.ps1 entrypoint. However, the scripts lack field-level semantic validation (priorities, field casing, timestamp consistency).

2. **Data/Pages/ has accumulated ~70+ audit documents** in `Data/Pages/audits/` from June-July 2026. This is a valuable historical corpus, but the sheer volume (~70 MB of audit outputs) may obscure the authoritative wiki content. Consider whether audits should rotate out or be summarized into a single living index.

3. **MemorySmith.Core/Docs/ is a mix of stale and current.** The `Plans/` directory contains 12+ plan documents, most superseded by `MemorySmith_FinalRefactorDesign_20260507.md`. The `ProgressReports/` (15+ files, April 2025) and `Reviews/` (20+ files, April 2024) are almost entirely historical. Only `Prompts/` (5 files) appears actively maintained.

4. **Training/ and Benchmarks/ are sparsely populated.** `Data/Training/` has corpora and exports subdirectories but no immediately obvious active pipeline. `Data/Benchmarks/` has a single `code-search-scorecard.json`.
